Shared server

Keeps scans, records, the lab log, customer folders and the shared log on your team's server, so everyone works from the same data, from anywhere. Your settings go there too, seen only by you. A worksheet goes there, with its photos, when you save it; one still open stays on this phone. It keeps working with no connection: changes are sent once the server can be reached.

Storage

Signed in to a shared server, this phone keeps all cert data, the records it saved, its own lab log trips and everything still out of the lab, so it works with no signal. Everyone else's records and trips show while online.
